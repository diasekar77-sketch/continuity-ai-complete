# Playback Troubleshooting Guide (SAMPLE - fictional, for demonstration)

## 1. Playback stops on Android
Ask the customer to restart the app and clear the app cache. If playback still stops, ask them to update the app to the latest version and check that battery optimisation is not restricting the app.

## 2. Reinstall step
If the earlier steps fail, ask the customer to uninstall and reinstall the app, then sign in again. Record every step the customer has already tried in the case notes so that it is not repeated.

## 3. Escalation to the technical team
When restart, cache clearing, update and reinstall have all been completed and playback still stops, escalate to the technical team with the device model, Android version and app version.

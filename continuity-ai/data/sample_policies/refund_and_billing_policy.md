# Refund and Billing Policy (SAMPLE - fictional, for demonstration)

## 1. Duplicate charges
If a customer is charged more than once for the same subscription period, the duplicate payment is eligible for a full refund once the billing team verifies both transactions in the payment system. Agents must not confirm or approve the refund to the customer until billing verification is complete.

## 2. Verification timeline and reference numbers
Billing verification usually takes 5 to 7 business days. When a case is raised with the billing team, the agent must share the billing reference number with the customer within one business day.

## 3. Refund processing
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days of approval.

## 4. Goodwill gestures
Goodwill credits, such as a free month of subscription, may only be offered by senior agents and require manager approval. Junior agents must not promise or suggest goodwill credits to customers.

## 5. Overdue follow-ups
If a promised follow-up is overdue, the agent must apologise, review the full case history, and give the customer a status update within 24 hours. Agents must not ask the customer to repeat information already recorded in the case.
